# external/reorderable

## Role

`external/reorderable` provides drag-and-drop reorder behavior for Compose lists.

## Module Type

- Android library

## Ownership

- Vendored support module

## Why It Matters

It is mainly a UI affordance dependency used in editors where the order of actions, conditions, or items matters.

## Reading Outcome

Low priority unless you are modifying reorder interactions in the UI.
